# SUMP PUMP PANEL — QUICK CARD

*Print this, laminate it, stick it on or next to the panel.*
*Full instructions: USB stick in the documentation drawer + printed manual.*

---

## What this box does
It runs the **two sump pumps automatically** — one at a time, taking turns. It
watches them and warns you if something is wrong. You normally do **nothing**.

---

## If you see / hear THIS → do THIS

| Signal | What it means | What to do |
|--------|---------------|-----------|
| 🔴🔊 **Big red light + SIREN** | **EMERGENCY** — water is too high, or both pumps are out. | Press **SILENCE** to quiet the siren. **Call the plumber NOW** (below). |
| 🔴 **Big red light, NO siren** | A problem the system is handling on its own (one pump is in trouble; the other is covering). | Not urgent. **Call the plumber soon.** |
| **Pump FAULT light STEADY** | That pump tripped; it will try again automatically. | Watch it. If it keeps happening, call the plumber. |
| **Pump FAULT light BLINKING** | That pump has given up (too many failures). | Get the pump fixed, then press **RESET**. |
| A **water-level light BLINKING** | A float switch looks broken. | Call the plumber; the system keeps working meanwhile. |
| Nothing lit, pumps quiet | All normal. | Nothing. |

---

## The two buttons

- **SILENCE** — turns the **siren** off. The red light stays on. Use it so you can think; it does **not** fix anything.
- **RESET** — press **after a pump has been repaired** to put it back in service. If the problem isn't fixed, it will just fault again.

## The selector switches (one per pump) — leave on **AUTO**
- **AUTO** = normal (leave it here).
- **OFF** = that pump is disabled.
- **MANUAL** = forces that pump to run now (only for a technician testing it).

## DO NOT
- ❌ Do **not** ignore the siren — high water can flood.
- ❌ Do **not** try to force both pumps on together (they share one pipe).
- ❌ Do **not** touch the mains/power wiring — that's an **electrician's** job.
